🇮🇳 India Travel Guide

Discover the beauty of India

🕌 Taj Mahal, Agra

UNESCO Heritage

Duniya ka sabse sundar prem ka pratik. Safed sangmarmar se bana yeh mahal Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan ne banvaya tha.

🏖️ Goa

Beach

India ka sabse famous beach destination. Sundar samudra tataye, Portuguese culture aur behtareen khaana.

🏔️ Manali

Mountains

Himachal Pradesh mein sthit yeh jagah adventure lovers ke liye paradise hai. Barfili pahariyan aur sundar nazare.

🕍 Varanasi

Spiritual

Duniya ka sabse purana shahar. Ganga ghat par sham ki aarti ek adbhut anubhav hai.

🏯 Jaipur

Pink City

Rajasthan ki rajdhani jo apne mahalOn aur kileOn ke liye mashoor hai. Amber Fort aur Hawa Mahal must visit hain.

🌴 Kerala

God's Own Country

Backwaters, houseboat, aur harit prakriti ke liye mashoor. Ayurvedic massage aur seafood ka anand lo.
